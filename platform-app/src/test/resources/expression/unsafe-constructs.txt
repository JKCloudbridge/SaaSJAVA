@@ The corpus of unsafe constructs (Sprint 13, ADR-0081).
@@
@@ One construct per line: the text of an expression, a TAB, then why it is unsafe. Every line must be rejected at parse
@@ or check time with an expression error that names a place, and nothing may be executed (UnsafeCorpusTest). A line that
@@ starts with two at signs is a comment. In the text, the sequence backslash-u followed by four hex digits stands for
@@ that character, so that invisible and look-alike characters can be written down and seen. To add a line: write the
@@ construct, a TAB and the reason; to see that the test can fail, follow "Sprint 13 manual testing.md".
@@
